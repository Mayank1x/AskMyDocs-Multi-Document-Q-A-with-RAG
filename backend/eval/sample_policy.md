# TechNova Corp Employee Leave Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

## 1. Annual Leave (PTO)
All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of Paid Time Off (PTO) per calendar year. 
- Employees gain 1.66 days of PTO per month.
- Up to 5 unused PTO days can roll over to the next calendar year.
- PTO requests exceeding 3 consecutive days must be approved by the department manager at least two weeks in advance.

## 2. Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days annually. 
- A doctor's note is required if an employee is out sick for more than 3 consecutive days.
- Sick leave does not roll over to the next year.

## 3. Parental Leave
TechNova offers 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave for primary caregivers and 8 weeks for secondary caregivers following the birth or adoption of a child.
- Employees must have worked at TechNova for at least 6 months to be eligible.

## 4. Bereavement Leave
Employees are granted up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave in the event of the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling).

## 5. Public Holidays
TechNova observes the following 8 public holidays:
New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, Day after Thanksgiving, and Christmas Day.
